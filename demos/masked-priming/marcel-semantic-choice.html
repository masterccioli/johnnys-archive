Masked Semantic Choice · Classroom Demonstration
12 trials · 6 words + 6 nonwords

Did you see a word?

Look at the center of the screen. A word or letter string will flash very briefly, followed by a mask. For each trial, answer two questions: which visible target is more related to what flashed, and whether a real word appeared.

Even if you saw nothing clearly, make your best guess. Both questions use one click each, then the next trial starts automatically.

Keys: A or ← for the left answer, L or → for the right answer. You can also click either answer. Prime exposure is approximate and depends on your monitor, browser and projector.

Teaching note: The six original semantic pairs have been retained. Six pseudoword trials provide word-presence controls. Forced semantic choices on pseudoword trials are recorded but not scored, because a nonword has no intended semantic associate.
